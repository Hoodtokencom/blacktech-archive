1 Executive Summary

Blacktech Solutions Corp operates a ten-property digital ecosystem spanning contractor matching, search, education, video, crypto, community networking, energy enrollment, social media, and business organization. Each platform serves a distinct purpose but feeds into one unified funnel: educate → reward → connect → contract → save → repeat.

This plan provides actionable, platform-specific SEO, content, and growth tactics for every property. The goal is maximum organic exposure supplemented by targeted paid campaigns, grassroots community partnerships, and a disciplined 30/60/90-day execution cadence.

Core Thesis
Own the search results for Black contractors, energy savings, and AI education in Chicago's south suburbs. Every platform must rank for its core keywords, cross-link to every other platform, and funnel users toward a conversion event — a course signup, a token reward, a contractor lead, a ComEd enrollment, or a network join.

4 Organic Growth Strategy

Organic exposure is the foundation. Every platform must be optimized for search, cross-promoted, and fed with consistent content before any paid dollars are spent.

SEO Foundation (All Platforms)

  • Google Search Console: Verify all 10 domains/subdomains. Submit XML sitemaps. Monitor index coverage and keyword rankings monthly.
  • Bing Webmaster Tools: Submit all sitemaps. Bing drives ~10% of search traffic and is underutilized by competitors.
  • Schema markup: Every platform gets JSON-LD structured data (Organization, LocalBusiness, Course, VideoObject, Service, FAQPage, Article). This is the #1 organic lever.
  • Page speed: All platforms must pass Core Web Vitals. Compress images, lazy-load videos, minify CSS/JS. Use Google PageSpeed Insights to audit monthly.
  • Internal linking: Every page on every platform links to at least 2 other Blacktech platforms. Build a "Blacktech Ecosystem" footer widget on all properties.
  • Local SEO: Command Center + NextcallPro + ComEd must have optimized Google Business Profiles with weekly posts, photos, and review responses.

Cross-Promotion Engine

  • Universal footer: All 10 platforms share a common footer with links to every other platform (icon + name + one-line description).
  • Email signatures: All outbound emails include 3 rotating platform CTAs (e.g., "Take a free Academy course", "Find a Black contractor on HoodSearch", "Save on your ComEd bill").
  • Social bios: All social profiles link to a linktree-style page on Command Center that lists all 10 platforms.
  • QR codes: Every printed material (flyers, business cards, invoices) includes QR codes to 2-3 platforms. Generate via Python qrcode library.

Content Funnel

  • Top of funnel (awareness): Academy courses, LiveVue videos, blog posts answering "what is" questions. Target: "free AI course", "energy education", "what is blockchain".
  • Mid funnel (consideration): HoodSearch contractor profiles, ComEd savings calculator, Academy certificates. Target: "find a contractor", "reduce electric bill", "Black contractor near me".
  • Bottom funnel (conversion): NextcallPro lead forms, ComEd signup, HBO membership, HOOD token wallet. Target: "hire electrician", "ComEd enrollment", "join business network".

Google Business Profile Posts (Weekly)

  • Monday: Academy course spotlight (with link to course page)
  • Wednesday: Project photo / before-after (link to NextcallPro)
  • Friday: Energy savings tip (link to ComEd signup)
  • Monthly: Platform announcement (new feature, new course, new token utility)

Nextdoor Strategy

  • Create Nextdoor business page for Blacktech Solutions Corp (electrical contractor category).
  • Post weekly in south suburbs neighborhoods (Dolton, Calumet City, Harvey, South Holland, Chicago Heights, Lansing, Glenwood).
  • Content: energy tips, "did you know" electrical safety, ComEd program reminders, contractor availability announcements.
  • Respond to every "need a recommendation" post for electricians/contractors in target neighborhoods.
  • Run Nextdoor local ads for ComEd signup ($50/week budget).

6 Partnerships & Community Outreach

Grassroots, in-person exposure builds trust that digital ads cannot. Every flyer, every church bulletin, every library rack plants a seed.

Library Flyer Program

  • Target libraries: Dolton Public, Calumet City Public, Harvey Public, Chicago Heights Public, South Holland Public, Lansing Public, Markham Public.
  • Flyer types: (1) Academy course flyer with QR code, (2) ComEd energy savings flyer with QR code, (3) HoodSearch contractor directory flyer, (4) Next/SSBN business network flyer.
  • Cadence: Drop 50 flyers per library, refresh monthly. Track QR code scans per location to measure ROI.
  • Design: Orange/green marketing palette (dark #0a0a0f background, #f59e0b orange + #69BE28 green). Tear-off tabs with phone number + short URL. See library-outreach-flyer reference in the document-templates skill.

Church Outreach

  • Identify 10-15 south side churches with active community outreach programs.
  • Offer free Academy course enrollment for church youth programs (AI + energy education).
  • Place ComEd energy savings flyers in church bulletins/community boards.
  • Sponsor a "Community Energy Day" — free energy assessment signups at church events.
  • Offer church members HOOD token rewards for completing Academy courses.

Contractor Referral Program

  • Every NextcallPro contractor gets a referral code. When they refer a homeowner who books a job, they earn HOOD tokens.
  • Contractors listed on HoodSearch get a "Verified by Blacktech" badge.
  • Contractor onboarding kit: HoodSearch profile setup + NextcallPro account + Academy course access + ComEd enrollment authority.
  • Monthly contractor mixer (in-person + LiveVue live stream) to build the network.

Community Centers

  • Partner with south side community centers (Park District field houses, YMCA branches, community resource centers).
  • Host free "Energy Savings Workshop" — 1-hour session, Academy course preview + ComEd signup on-site.
  • Provide Academy course access kiosks (tablet + QR code) at community center lobbies.
  • Co-sponsor STEM/AI education nights for youth — Academy courses as curriculum.
