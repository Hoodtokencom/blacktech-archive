What is home electrification?

It means replacing gas-powered appliances — your furnace, water heater, stove, dryer — with high-efficiency electric ones.

ComEd wants more homes on electric power because it's cleaner, more efficient, and reduces strain on the gas grid during deep freezes.

💡 ComEd offers rebates and incentives that make switching nearly free.
🏠⚡🌱

You get a modernized home, lower energy bills, and no more gas bills at all — all coordinated by a licensed electrician who handles the paperwork.
