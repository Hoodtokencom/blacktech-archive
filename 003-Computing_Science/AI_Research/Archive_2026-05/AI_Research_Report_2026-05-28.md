# AI Research Report

**Report Date:** May 28, 2026

---

## 1. Anthropic Claude 4 (Sonnet & Opus)
- **Category:** Foundation Model / LLM
- **What It Does:** The latest Claude release introduces an "extended thinking" mode for complex reasoning, supports up to 1M tokens of context, and adds native computer-use capabilities that let the model control a desktop environment to complete multi-step tasks.
- **Why It's Interesting:** Claude 4 shows strong performance on SWE-bench and legal reasoning benchmarks, positioning it as a top-tier choice for enterprise workflows and autonomous software engineering agents.
- **Source/Link:** https://anthropic.com

---

## 2. Runway Gen-4
- **Category:** Generative AI / Video
- **What It Does:** Runway's next-generation video synthesis model generates cinematic 1080p clips with precise camera motion controls, consistent character identity, and physics-aware scene composition from text and image inputs.
- **Why It's Interesting:** Gen-4 narrows the gap between AI-generated and professionally shot footage, offering fine-grained director-style controls that filmmakers can integrate into production pipelines.
- **Source/Link:** https://runwayml.com

---

## 3. Cursor AI 1.0 (General Availability)
- **Category:** Dev Tool / Coding AI
- **What It Does:** The AI-native code editor officially launched out of beta with autonomous "Composer" mode that can plan, write, and refactor entire codebases across multiple files using natural-language instructions.
- **Why It's Interesting:** Cursor 1.0 turns the editor into an autonomous junior developer, capable of understanding large monorepos and executing multi-step engineering tasks with minimal supervision.
- **Source/Link:** https://cursor.com

---

## 4. Meta Llama 4 (Multimodal)
- **Category:** Open Source / Foundation Model
- **What It Does:** Meta released an open-weights multimodal model that natively processes text, images, and video within a single transformer architecture, featuring a 500K context window and improved reasoning over long documents.
- **Why It's Interesting:** Llama 4 closes the capability gap with closed frontier models while remaining free for commercial use, giving startups and researchers unrestricted access to state-of-the-art multimodal AI.
- **Source/Link:** https://llama.meta.com

---

## 5. Google DeepMind AlphaFold Server Expansion
- **Category:** Healthcare AI / Science
- **What It Does:** The public AlphaFold web platform expanded to predict DNA and RNA complex structures, ligand binding, and antibody interactions, making advanced structural biology accessible from any browser.
- **Why It's Interesting:** By opening these capabilities to non-computational biologists, it accelerates drug discovery and synthetic biology research without requiring HPC infrastructure.
- **Source/Link:** https://alphafoldserver.com

## 6. ElevenLabs AI Phone Agent
- **Category:** Voice AI / Enterprise
- **What It Does:** A turnkey platform that lets businesses deploy human-sounding AI voice agents for inbound and outbound phone calls, complete with CRM integrations, appointment booking, and real-time call analytics.
- **Why It's Interesting:** The latency and emotional expressiveness of the voices make interactions nearly indistinguishable from human agents, potentially reshaping the $500B global call-center industry.
- **Source/Link:** https://elevenlabs.io

## 7. Figure AI Figure 02 Production Release
- **Category:** Robotics
- **What It Does:** Figure's second-generation humanoid robot entered commercial production for logistics and retail, featuring improved dexterity, a 5-hour battery pack, and a new natural-language task interface.
- **Why It's Interesting:** Figure 02 represents one of the first general-purpose humanoid robots actually shipping to enterprise customers for real warehouse and shelf-stocking workflows.

## 8. Mistral AI Codestral 2
- **Category:** Dev Tool / Coding AI
- **What It Does:** A specialized code generation model supporting 80+ programming languages with a 256K context window, built-in test generation, and a new "architect mode" for planning system design before writing code.
- **Why It's Interesting:** Codestral 2 offers a lightweight, self-hostable alternative to cloud-based coding assistants, with performance that rivals larger models on software engineering benchmarks.
- **Source/Link:** https://mistral.ai

## 9. LangChain LangGraph Platform
- **Category:** Dev Tool / Framework
- **What It Does:** A managed cloud service for deploying agentic AI workflows, offering visual DAG builders, built-in human-in-the-loop checkpoints, and native integrations with major LLM providers and vector databases.
- **Why It's Interesting:** LangGraph Platform lowers the barrier to productionizing multi-agent systems, giving developers observability and state management tools that were previously custom-built in-house.
- **Source/Link:** https://langchain.com/langgraph

---

## 10. NVIDIA Digits Desktop AI Supercomputer
- **Category:** AI Hardware / Edge
- **What It Does:** A $3,000 personal AI supercomputer built on GB10 Grace Blackwell architecture, capable of running 200B parameter models locally and fine-tuning 70B models on a desktop workstation.
- **Why It's Interesting:** Digits brings data-center-class training and inference to individual developers and small labs, enabling private, on-premise AI development without cloud dependency or data privacy concerns.
- **Source/Link:** https://nvidia.com/digits

## Quick Trends Summary

The May 28 landscape is defined by **production-grade autonomy**: AI tools are moving from impressive demos to shipped products—whether it's humanoid robots in warehouses, AI coders editing entire repos, or voice agents handling real customer calls. The second clear trend is **local-first AI**, exemplified by NVIDIA Digits and open models like Llama 4 and Codestral 2, as enterprises increasingly demand data sovereignty alongside frontier capability.
