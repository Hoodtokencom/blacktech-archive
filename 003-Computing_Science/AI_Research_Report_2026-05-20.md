# AI Research Report

**Report Date:** May 20, 2026

---

## 1. Meta Llama 4
- **Category:** Generative AI / Open Source
- **What It Does:** A new family of open-weights models (Scout, Maverick, and Behemoth) that natively support multimodal understanding across text, image, and video, with a 10M-token context window in the smaller variants and MoE architecture for efficient inference.
- **Why It's Interesting:** It is Meta’s most capable open release to date, directly challenging closed frontier models while letting developers fine-tune and self-host a near-state-of-the-art multimodal engine without API lock-in.
- **Source/Link:** https://ai.meta.com/blog/llama-4-multimodal-intelligence/

---

## 2. Anthropic Claude 4
- **Category:** Generative AI / Productivity
- **What It Does:** The next major iteration of Claude, shipping in Sonnet and Opus variants, featuring extended thinking modes, advanced tool use across long documents, and significantly improved coding and agentic task performance.
- **Why It's Interesting:** Claude 4 extends Anthropic’s lead in long-context reliability and safe agentic execution, making it the preferred backend for complex legal, medical, and software workflows that require sustained reasoning.
- **Source/Link:** https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-4

---

## 3. xAI Grok 3
- **Category:** Generative AI / Multimodal
- **What It Does:** Elon Musk’s xAI released Grok 3 with a native voice mode, real-time search integration via X, and a large-scale multimodal architecture designed to compete with the top-tier closed models on math, coding, and world-knowledge benchmarks.
- **Why It's Interesting:** By tightly coupling the model to live X data and offering it inside Tesla vehicles and the X app, xAI is building a vertically integrated AI ecosystem that leverages real-time social discourse as a differentiator.
- **Source/Link:** https://x.ai/blog/grok-3

## 4. Google Gemini 2.5 Pro
- **Category:** Generative AI / Reasoning
- **What It Does:** Google’s flagship reasoning model, featuring a 1–2 million token context window, deep research agent capability, and enhanced multimodal logic across video, audio, and codebase analysis.
- **Why It's Interesting:** Its ability to ingest entire video lectures, feature-length films, or massive code repositories in a single pass makes it uniquely suited for enterprise knowledge extraction and scientific literature review.
- **Source/Link:** https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/gemini-2-5-pro/

---

## 5. Runway Gen-4
- **Category:** Generative AI / Video
- **What It Does:** Runway’s latest video generation suite produces high-fidelity, coherent cinematic scenes with improved character consistency, camera motion control, and physics-aware environmental interactions from text and image prompts.
- **Why It's Interesting:** It dramatically narrows the gap between AI-generated clips and production-ready footage, allowing indie filmmakers and creative studios to storyboard and iterate scenes in hours rather than weeks.
- **Source/Link:** https://runwayml.com/blog/introducing-runway-gen-4/

---

## 6. ElevenLabs Scribe
- **Category:** Speech AI / Developer Tool
- **What It Does:** A production-grade speech-to-text model that claims best-in-class word-error rate across dozens of languages, speaker diarization, and real-time streaming transcription suitable for meetings, podcasts, and call centers.
- **Why It's Interesting:** By combining voice cloning, TTS, and now best-in-class STT in one platform, ElevenLabs is becoming the default audio stack for AI-native applications, from voice agents to accessibility tools.
- **Source/Link:** https://elevenlabs.io/blog/scribe-speech-to-text

---

## 7. Replit Agent
- **Category:** Dev Tool / AI Agent
- **What It Does:** An autonomous coding agent embedded inside Replit’s cloud IDE that can plan architectures, write full-stack code, configure deployments, and fix bugs across multiple files from natural language descriptions.
- **Why It's Interesting:** Unlike isolated code completers, Replit Agent owns the entire lifecycle—from repo creation to hosting—making it one of the most complete end-to-end "vibe coding" experiences for non-experts and rapid prototyping.
- **Source/Link:** https://replit.com/blog/replit-agent

---

## 8. Perplexity Assistant
- **Category:** Consumer AI / Productivity
- **What It Does:** A mobile-first AI assistant that combines Perplexity’s answer engine with voice interaction, calendar and email tool use, and on-device actions to handle scheduling, research, and writing tasks across iOS and Android.
- **Why It's Interesting:** It challenges native phone assistants by offering transparent sourcing, deep web search, and multi-step task execution, positioning Perplexity as a daily-driver utility rather than just a search engine.
- **Source/Link:** https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/introducing-perplexity-assistant

---

## 9. Qwen3 by Alibaba Cloud
- **Category:** Generative AI / Open Source
- **What It Does:** The third generation of Alibaba’s Qwen model family, shipping dense and mixture-of-experts variants up to hundreds of billions of parameters, with strong multilingual performance, agentic tool use, and permissive open-weight licensing.
- **Why It's Interesting:** Qwen3 continues the trend of frontier-capable models coming from Chinese labs with fully open weights, driving down inference costs globally and giving enterprises a privacy-friendly alternative to Western APIs.
- **Source/Link:** https://qwenlm.github.io/blog/qwen3/

---

## 10. Physical Intelligence π0
- **Category:** Robotics / Foundation Model
- **What It Does:** A generalist vision-language-action model trained to control diverse robot embodiments, enabling humanoid and robotic arms to perform novel manipulation tasks—from folding laundry to assembling components—via imitation learning and self-improvement.
- **Why It's Interesting:** π0 is one of the first "general-purpose robot brains" that transfers across hardware, suggesting a future where downloading a foundation model is as common for robots as it is for chatbots today.
- **Source/Link:** https://www.physicalintelligence.company/blog/pi0

## Quick Trends Summary

At the same time, **multimodality is becoming table stakes**—every major release now handles text, image, audio, and video natively, setting the stage for truly integrated digital assistants.
