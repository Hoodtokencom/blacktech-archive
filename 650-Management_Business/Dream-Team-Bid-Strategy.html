THE 7-PHASE PLAYBOOK

1. INTELLIGENCE (Days 1-3)
Monitor SAM.gov, PBC, CDOT, IDOT, MWRD, CDB daily. Know what's funded before it hits the street.
2. GO / NO-GO (Day 3)
Score: Technical fit (30%), Past performance (20%), Agency relationship (15%), Competition (15%), Margin (20%). Below 3.0 = walk.
3. TEAMING (Days 3-5)
Prime vs sub. Never bid alone if a teammate's cert boosts the score. Teaming agreement ready in 24 hrs.
4. ESTIMATING (Days 5-10)
Two cost models: Aggressive (win price) and Safe (walk price). Labor burden 35-40%, contingency 5-10%.
5. PROPOSAL (Days 7-12)
Capability statement, 3-5 past performance examples, 3 win themes. Nobody writes alone — PM drafts, Derrell reviews.
6. SUBMISSION (Day 12-14)
Submit 48 hrs before deadline. Bond + COI ready. Checklist: forms, bond, COI, references, MBE/WBE docs.
7. POST-BID (Day 14+)
ALWAYS request debrief. Won = what sealed it. Lost = what killed it. Update War Room.
