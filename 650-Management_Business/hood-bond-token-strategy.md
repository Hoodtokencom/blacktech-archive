## Worthy Referral Program Details
- **Double-sided:** Both referrer + referee get free $10 bond
- **Limit:** 50 bonds/year ($500) per referrer
- **Requirements:** Must hold 1 purchased bond, US citizen, 18+
- **Restrictions:** No paid ads, no spam, no extra incentives beyond Worthy's offer
- **HOOD tokens are SEPARATE** from Worthy's referral — they're your add-on, not Worthy's incentive
- **Link:** worthybonds.com/referrals (terms page)

## Legal Considerations
- **HOOD as utility token** (trading, Academy, rewards) = NOT a security
- **Bond-backed HOOD with yield** = security (SEC Howey Test)
- **Two-token model recommended:**
  - HOOD Loyalty (utility, no yield claim) — current model
  - HOOD Bond Token (security, backed by bonds, pays yield) — future, Reg A+
- **For now:** Keep HOOD as utility, bonds are separate product, tokens are "free with bond purchase"
- **Key:** Don't market HOOD as "backed by bonds" until Reg A+ filed — say "earn HOOD when you invest"
