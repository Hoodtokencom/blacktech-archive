# Clean Energy Jobs and Justice Fund (CEJJF) — Fair Path Program

## Organization
- **Name:** Clean Energy Jobs and Justice Fund (CEJJF)
- **Type:** Illinois' nonprofit green bank
- **Created by:** Climate and Equitable Jobs Act (CEJA)
- **Address:** 7234 W North Ave, Ste 208, Chicago, IL 60707
- **Website:** cleanenergyjobsjustice.org
- **Managed by:** Public Sector Consultants (PSC) — nonpartisan public policy firm

## Mission
Expand access to capital for clean energy businesses that face barriers to traditional financing. Help homeowners and small businesses lower energy costs. Facilitate clean energy solutions for frontline communities. Foster healthier environments and stronger local economies. Focus on MBE contractors, contractors of color, low-income, EJ, and BIPOC communities.

## Fair Path — Home Energy Savings Loan Program
From presentation slides (Jul 29 2026 meeting):

**Why Become an Approved Contractor?**
1. **No cost for Fair Path** — free to participate
2. **Expand your client base** through more flexible financing
3. **Potential access to line of credit equal to 30% of total job cost**
4. **Apply at:** cleanenergyjobsjustice.org

**Fair Path = CEJJF + SELF Partnership**
- CEJJF partnered with **Solar and Energy Loan Fund (SELF)** to vet, approve, and pay contractors
- SELF has sole discretion to vet, approve, and pay for work performed for Sustain Energy Finance clients
- Consumer-facing platform: **canopyclimate.com** (SELF's tool for homeowners)
- CanopyClimate helps homeowners: plan upgrades, find rebates/tax credits, compare costs vs savings, find contractors, apply for loans

**Fair Path Contractor Application Form:**
- URL: https://www.tfaforms.com/5198750
- Title: "Clean Energy Jobs and Justice Fund Contractor Application"
- Powered by TFAForms (FormAssembly)

**Eligibility Requirements:**
1. Current General Liability Insurance certificate
2. Workers Compensation Insurance certificate (if available)
3. Signed W-9
4. Current State Construction Specialty License, OR current local Construction License (if required)
5. Must acknowledge Terms and Conditions
