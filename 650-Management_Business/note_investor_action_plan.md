## The Pitch

### One-Liner
"I invest in performing real estate notes secured by residential property at 12%+ yield. You fund, I find and manage the notes, we split returns. Minimum $25K."

### Key Numbers
- 12%+ annualized yield (vs 4% CD, 8% stocks)
- 60-70% LTV (equity cushion protects downside)
- Monthly income (borrower pays principal + interest)
- Secured by real estate (tangible collateral)
- SDIRA-eligible (tax-free/deferred 12% returns)

### Objection: "What if borrower stops paying?"
→ "Note is at 65% LTV. If they default, we foreclose and take back property worth 40%+ more than our investment. Worst case = we acquire real estate at a deep discount."

### Objection: "12% sounds too good"
→ "We buy notes at a discount. $100K note at 6% interest, purchased for $75K = 12% yield. The discount creates the yield."
