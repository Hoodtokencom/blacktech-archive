# 910 - Regional Data (Market Data)

> **CRITICAL:** These lists are core market data for Blacktech Solutions. They represent the customer base, partner network, and lead pipeline. Every business, church, contractor, and school listed here is a potential client, collaborator, or commission opportunity.
>
> **Call Center:** These lists feed the Blacktech call center — every entry is a call target. Data quality = call conversion rate. Keep names, phone numbers, and addresses accurate and current.

Directory of regional entities by location — businesses, churches, contractors, schools, community organizations, etc.

## Structure
Organize by geography:
- `chicago/` — Chicago neighborhoods
- `cook-county/` — Cook County (outside Chicago)
- `illinois/` — Statewide
- `regional/` — Multi-state / Midwest region

Each location subfolder contains:
- Business directories (name, type, address, contact)
- Church directories
- Contractor listings
- School listings
- Community organizations
- Service directories (electrical, HVAC, plumbing, solar, battery, energy consulting, construction, etc.)
- Count summaries

## Naming Convention
Files: `{area}_{type}.json` or `{area}_{type}.csv`
Example: `chicago-south_businesses.json`, `cook-county_churches.csv`
