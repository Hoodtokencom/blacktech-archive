## 1. THE PLAYBOOK — 7 PHASES OF EVERY BID

### Phase 1: INTELLIGENCE (Days 1-3)
- Monitor SAM.gov, PBC, CDOT, IDOT, MWRD, CDB procurement portals DAILY
- Set up saved searches on SAM.gov by NAICS codes:
  - 238210 (Electrical Contractors)
  - 238220 (Plumbing/HVAC)
  - 423610 (Electrical Apparatus)
- Track agency budgets — know what's funded BEFORE it hits the street
- Build relationships with procurement officers (call, don't just email)
- **Owner:** Each PM monitors their assigned agencies

### Phase 2: GO / NO-GO DECISION (Day 3)
Score every opportunity on 5 factors:

| Factor | Weight | Score 1-5 |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Technical fit (can we actually do it?) | 30% | |
| Past performance match (done similar work?) | 20% | |
| Relationship with agency (do we know anyone?) | 15% | |
| Competition level (how many bidders?) | 15% | |
| Margin potential (is it worth it?) | 20% | |

- **Score < 3.0 average = NO-GO** — walk away, save your resources
- **Score 3.0-4.0 = GO with caution** — team up, reduce risk
- **Score 4.0+ = GO hard** — commit full resources

### Phase 3: TEAMING (Days 3-5)
- Identify prime vs sub roles — we don't always have to be prime
- Leverage certifications as the edge:
  - **Fiona (PZS Concierge):** MBE/WBE/DBE certified + Notary — HUGE advantage on federal set-asides
  - **Blacktech:** EESP, EV, DG certified — energy specialty nobody else has
  - **15 CEJA contractors:** bench depth for labor-heavy projects
- Teaming agreement template ready to sign in 24 hours
- **Rule:** Never bid alone if a teammate's certification boosts the score

### Phase 4: ESTIMATING (Days 5-10)
- Pull permits and historical data on similar projects
- Get vendor quotes EARLY — don't wait until the last week
- Build TWO cost models:
  - **Aggressive** (win price) — thin margin, competitive
  - **Safe** (walk price) — full margin, no regrets if we lose
- Factor in:
  - Labor burden (insurance, taxes, benefits) — 35-40% on top of base
  - Material escalation (especially copper, steel)
  - Bond costs (1-2% of contract value)
  - Contingency (5-10% minimum)
- **Owner:** Derrell reviews ALL estimates before submission

### Phase 5: PROPOSAL WRITING (Days 7-12)
- Capability Statement (1-page) — always attached
- Past performance matrix — 3-5 relevant projects with:
  - Contract value
  - Agency
  - Scope
  - Completion date
  - Reference contact
- Win themes — 3 bullet points that answer "Why us?"
  - Example: "Only bidder with EESP certification — guaranteed energy savings"
  - Example: "MBE/WBE team with 15 licensed electricians on the bench"
- **Rule:** Nobody writes a proposal alone. PM drafts, Derrell reviews, second PM proofreads.

### Phase 6: SUBMISSION (Day 12-14)
- Submit 48 hours before deadline (not the night before)
- Confirm receipt with procurement officer (email + call)
- Keep a copy of everything submitted
- Bond + COI ready to go (don't let these hold you up)
- **Checklist before submit:**
  - [ ] All forms signed
  - [ ] Bond attached (if required)
  - [ ] COI current (6+ months remaining)
  - [ ] Past performance references verified
  - [ ] MBE/WBE/DBE paperwork (if applicable)
  - [ ] Page limits respected
  - [ ] Deadline timestamp confirmed

### Phase 7: POST-BID (Day 14+)
- Debrief win or lose — ALWAYS request debrief
  - Won: What sealed it? (repeat in future bids)
  - Lost: What killed it? (fix and never repeat)
- Track win rate by agency, PM, and project type
- Update War Room dashboard with results
