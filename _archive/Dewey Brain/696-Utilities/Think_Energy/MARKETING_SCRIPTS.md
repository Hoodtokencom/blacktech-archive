## 4. Energy Audit Walkthrough Script

"Thanks for having me. Here's the plan:"
1. Check thermostat settings and type
2. Inspect insulation and draft points
3. Review appliance energy usage
4. Check lighting type (LED vs old bulbs)
5. Look at electrical panel capacity
6. Recommend smart switches, smart thermostat, LED retrofit

"Based on what I see, you can cut your bill by 30-40% with these 3 changes: [list]. I can connect you with a licensed electrician if you need panel upgrades or hardwired work done."
