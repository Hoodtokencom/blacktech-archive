## 1. SAM.gov — Federal Contractor Registration (UEI)

**URL:** https://sam.gov → "Register Entity"  
**Cost:** FREE  
**Processing Time:** 7–10 business days (up to 4–6 weeks with issues)  
**Renewal:** Annual (every 365 days)

### Required Documents
- IRS EIN confirmation letter (CP-575 or 147C)
- Illinois LLC Articles of Organization
- State of Illinois business license/registration
- Electrician contractor license
- Proof of physical address (utility bill, lease)
- Banking info for EFT (routing + account number, voided check)
- Notarized letter designating Entity Administrator

### NAICS Code
- **238210** — Electrical Contractors and Other Wiring Installation Contractors
- Small Business Size Standard: Under $19.5M annual revenue

### Steps
1. Create Login.gov account at https://login.gov
2. Go to SAM.gov → "Register New Entity" → "U.S. entity"
3. Request Unique Entity ID (UEI) — replaced DUNS as of April 2022
4. Complete registration sections: Core Data, Assertions, Reps & Certs, Points of Contact, Financial Info
5. Submit notarized Entity Administrator letter
6. Await activation confirmation email (7–10 days)

### Key Contacts
- SAM Help Desk: (866) 606-8220
- SBA Illinois District Office: (312) 353-4520
