PURPOSE:
This Private Contract establishes a private contract irrevocable trust, 
faith-based, non-profit organization under the Common Law, to serve the 
spiritual, educational, and charitable needs of the Grantor’s family and 
community. The Trust exists for the purpose of identifying, accumulating, 
purchasing and holding all assets that become available, and providing for 
prudent administration and distribution of those assets for charitable, 
educational, and religious purposes.
