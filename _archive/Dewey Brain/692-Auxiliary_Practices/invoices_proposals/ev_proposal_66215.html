Section 5 — Category A: 2FM Fleet Facilities Technical Approach

Understanding of Scope

Blacktech Solutions Corp understands that Category A requires procurement, installation, configuration, and ongoing maintenance of EV charging stations at approximately 66 City-owned fleet facility sites over a 60-month contract term. The City (2FM) will provide all electrical wiring and infrastructure at each site; Blacktech will supply and install EVSE hardware, integrate with the AssetWorks FuelFocus platform, and provide 24/7 monitoring and support.

EVSE Hardware Partners

Blacktech Solutions Corp will source EVSE hardware from U.S.-based manufacturers meeting all City specifications. Our proposed hardware partners include established brands offering:

  • Level 2 AC Chargers: Min. 208V/30A, SAE J1772, dual-port, 15 ft. retractable cable, OCPP 1.6/2.0 compliant
  • Level 3 DC Fast Chargers: Min. 208V/180A, CHAdeMO + CCS connectors, ≥22 kWh capacity, cloud-managed
  • Solar Canopy Chargers: Min. 4 kW, 20'L × 10'W canopy, 100 mph wind rating, 8 ft. clearance, CHAdeMO + CCS

AssetWorks FuelFocus Integration

All installed EVSE units will be configured for full integration with the City's AssetWorks FuelFocus fleet management platform. Integration will deliver:

  • Real-time kWh consumption per vehicle/unit
  • Odometer and timestamp data capture at each charge event
  • Unit ID and site location tagging
  • Remote shutoff capability via networked software
  • API data feed compatible with AssetWorks reporting requirements

Section 6 — Category B: CDOT Public Charging Technical Approach

Understanding of Scope

Category B requires a fully turnkey solution across 7 identified CDOT public locations — including design, utility coordination, civil and electrical construction, EVSE procurement, installation, commissioning, and ongoing operations & maintenance. Blacktech Solutions Corp will serve as the prime electrical contractor, self-performing all electrical work and partnering with civil/structural subcontractors as needed for canopy and site work.

Turnkey Delivery Approach

Phase 1 — Design & Permitting (Months 1–3)

  • Site survey and load analysis for each of the 7 locations
  • Electrical design drawings stamped by licensed Illinois PE
  • City of Chicago building and electrical permit applications
  • ComEd utility coordination — service upgrade requests, transformer sizing
  • ADA compliance review per ADAAG standards
  • Phase 1 Environmental Site Assessment where required

Phase 2 — Site Preparation & Construction (Months 3–9)

  • Electrical trenching and conduit installation (underground service runs)
  • Service entrance upgrades and panel installation
  • Transformer coordination with ComEd
  • Site prep: bollards, signage, ADA-compliant surfaces, lighting
  • Cable management and weatherproof enclosures
  • All work performed by EVITP-certified Illinois licensed electricians
  • Davis-Bacon prevailing wages paid to all workers

Phase 3 — EVSE Installation & Commissioning (Months 8–12)

  • EVSE hardware delivery and installation (pedestal/wall mount per site)
  • Network configuration, OCPI integration, and software commissioning
  • Load testing and ComEd final inspection
  • City inspection and certificate of occupancy/completion
  • Staff training for City personnel on charger operation and monitoring

Phase 4 — Operations & Maintenance (Months 12–60)

  • Minimum 80% station uptime guaranteed (target: 90%+)
  • Real-time monitoring: utilization, energy, uptime dashboards
  • Preventive maintenance schedule per City approval
  • Corrective maintenance within 4 hours (critical) / 24 hours (non-critical)
  • Annual uptime and performance reporting to CDOT
  • Decommissioning plan available at end of pilot

Buy America Compliance

Blacktech Solutions Corp commits to 100% Buy America compliance for all EVSE hardware: All charging stations will be sourced from U.S. manufacturers with domestic component content exceeding 55% of total component cost, in accordance with DOE Grant Term 45 and IIJA Buy America requirements. Documentation and manufacturer certifications will be provided prior to procurement.

Equity & Environmental Justice

Blacktech Solutions Corp strongly supports CDOT's equity goals for the EV Charging Pilot Program. Our team will prioritize:

  • Hiring local Chicago residents for construction and maintenance roles
  • Targeting South Side and West Side sites for community workforce development
  • Partnering with EVITP training programs to build local EV electrician capacity
  • Transparent community engagement during site construction

Cybersecurity Controls

  • All EVSE units OCPP 1.6/2.0 compliant with TLS 1.2+ encryption
  • Network segmentation between EVSE management and public internet
  • Firmware update management via vendor-managed secure channel
  • Incident response plan in place for cyber events
  • Annual penetration testing of EVSE management platform
