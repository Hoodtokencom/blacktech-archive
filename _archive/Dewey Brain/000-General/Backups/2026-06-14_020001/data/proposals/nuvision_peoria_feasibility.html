Executive Summary

Blacktech Solutions Corp proposes to manage a full multi-discipline engineering feasibility study for the rehabilitation of 7546–7548 S. Peoria Street — an 18-unit residential building in Chicago's Englewood neighborhood. This pre-construction phase establishes the technical foundation required to produce permit-ready working drawings and a reliable construction bid package for NuVision Development.

The scope includes coordinated site walks and stamped drawing sets across three engineering disciplines: Electrical, Mechanical, and Structural — along with Blacktech's project coordination, ComEd utility engagement, and final presentation to NuVision's development team.
