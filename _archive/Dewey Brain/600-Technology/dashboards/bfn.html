      💡 How HOOD Token Works
      🏘️
      Community Owned
      HOOD token belongs to the 60628 community. Every business, resident, and supporter can hold it. The more businesses that accept it, the more valuable it becomes.
      💸
      Spend Local, Earn Local
      Use HOOD to pay at participating businesses in the 60628 network. Businesses earn HOOD by joining the BFN monthly rotation. Keep dollars circulating in the hood.
      📅
      Monthly Industry Funding
      Each month's featured industry receives a HOOD distribution. Barbers in January, Dentists in February, Lounges in May — the community funds each other. No banks. No grants needed.
      🔗
      Built on Base (Ethereum L2)
      Base is a fast, low-cost blockchain backed by Coinbase. Sending HOOD costs pennies. No middlemen. Every transaction is transparent and permanent on-chain.
