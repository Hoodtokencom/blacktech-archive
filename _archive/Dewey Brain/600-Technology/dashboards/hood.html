💡 How HOOD Token Works
🏘️
Community Owned
HOOD belongs to the 60628 community. Every business, resident, and supporter can hold it. The more businesses that accept it, the more valuable it becomes.
💸
Spend Local, Earn Local
Use HOOD to pay at participating businesses in the 60628 network. Businesses earn HOOD by joining the BFN monthly rotation. Keep dollars circulating in the hood.
📅
Monthly Industry Funding
Each month's featured industry receives a HOOD distribution — Barbers in January, Dentists in February, Lounges in May. The community funds each other. No banks. No grants.
🔗
Built on Base (Ethereum L2)
Base is fast, low-cost, backed by Coinbase. Sending HOOD costs pennies. No middlemen. Every transaction is transparent and permanent on-chain.
