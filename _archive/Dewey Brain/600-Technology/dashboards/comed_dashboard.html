✅ Blacktech Solutions Corp is a registered ComEd Energy Efficiency Service Provider (EESP). You perform the actual installation work for the Whole Home Electric program.
🏠 ComEd Whole Home Electric — Free Programs
🌡️
Air Source Heat Pump
Replaces gas furnace. Works in Chicago sub-zero temps. Requires 240V circuit + panel capacity check.
FREE to customer 240V circuit required
❄️
Mini Split Heat Pump
Ductless option — ideal for additions, single rooms, or homes without ductwork.
FREE to customer 240V dedicated circuit
💧
Heat Pump Water Heater
Replaces gas or electric resistance water heater. 3–4x more efficient. Requires 240V, adequate space + clearance.
FREE to customer 240V dedicated circuit
🍳
Induction Stovetop
Replaces gas range. Cool surface, faster cooking, no open flame. All new cookware may be needed.
FREE to customer 40–50A 240V circuit
🔄
Electric Dryer
Replaces gas dryer. Heat pump dryer option available. Simple swap if 240V outlet exists.
FREE to customer 240V outlet required
🔌
Panel Upgrade (Blacktech Core Work)
Required for most installs. 100A → 200A upgrade enables all new appliances. This is Blacktech's primary electrical scope.
FREE (ComEd funded) BLACKTECH PERFORMS
🏠
Weatherization
Insulation, air sealing. Often bundled with heat pump install to ensure efficiency targets are met.
FREE to customer
🚗
EV Charger (Level 2)
EVSE installation. Requires 240V 50A dedicated circuit to garage or parking area.
FREE / Rebate 240V 50A circuit
📋 Eligibility Requirements
Income Limits (WHE Program)
Household 1: ≤ $54,360 / yr (80% AMI)
Household 2: ≤ $62,120 / yr
Household 3: ≤ $69,880 / yr
Household 4: ≤ $77,640 / yr
Funded by IL Climate & Equitable Jobs Act
Customer Requirements
✅ Adult (18+) present at all appointments
✅ Pets secured during assessment
✅ Access to HVAC, water heater, panel
✅ All rooms with major appliances accessible
✅ ComEd account holder
EESP Contractor (Blacktech) Requirements
✅ Licensed electrical contractor
✅ ComEd EESP registration active
✅ Before + after photos per job
✅ Equipment serial numbers documented
✅ Permit pulled for panel/circuit work
