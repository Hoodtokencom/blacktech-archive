## 1. Identity — What We Are

A **decentralized autonomous guild** for neighborhood-level blockchain education, hands-on project building, and ethical collective investment.

**Core Principles:**
- No single owner — membership is open and voluntary
- No corporate entity — operated through blockchain-native governance (DAO-style or community consensus)
- No centralized platform hostage — use permissionless technology
- Education first — learn before you invest
- Community wealth — pooled contributions stay controlled by the group

**Name Options (avoiding legal flags):**
- Neighborhood Blockchain Hub ✅
- Community Blockchain Guild ✅
- Decentralized Learning Circle ✅
- The Block Guild ✅
- Neighborhood Blockchain University ⚠️ (legal risk — "university" requires state accreditation if charging, even if free may draw scrutiny)

**Proposed Final Name:** **Neighborhood Blockchain Hub**

## 2. Structure — How It Works

### A. Decentralized Governance (No Single Boss)
- Decisions made by member vote (simple majority or consensus)
- Proposals submitted as posts in the group
- No president/CEO/leader in the traditional sense
- Derrell Black may be the founder/facilitator, but not the owner

### B. Open Membership (No Gatekeeping)
- Anyone can join by showing up
- No dues required for education track
- Optional investment pool requires contribution (skin in the game)
- Inclusive across age, background, technical skill

### C. Three Tracks (Learn → Build → Earn)

| Track | Purpose | Commitment |
|-------|---------|------------|
| **Education** | Learn wallets, crypto, DeFi, security, smart contracts | Weekly meetups; free |
| **Building** | Actually code/build: small dApps, NFT projects, local tools | Weekly workshops; contribute skills |
| **Investment** | Pool small contributions, vote on investments, share returns | Monthly contribution; vote required |

### D. Meeting Models
- **Digital:** Telegram group (already decentralized, encrypted)
- **Physical:** Your office, a community center, or rotating homes
- **Hybrid:** In-person building sessions + Telegram for async updates

## 3. Technology Stack (Decentralized Options)

| Function | Centralized (Old) | Decentralized (Guild) |
|----------|-------------------|-----------------------|
| Communication | Facebook group | Telegram Secret Chat / Matrix self-hosted |
| Voting | Show of hands | Snapshot.org (free blockchain voting) |
| Treasury/Payments | Bank account | Gnosis Safe (multi-signature crypto wallet) |
| Record keeping | Google Docs | IPFS or Arweave (permanent, uncensorable storage) |
| Website | WordPress | IPFS-hosted static site |
| Education content | YouTube | IPFS + community notes |

**Recommended for Start:**
- **Telegram** — already working, private, easy
- **Snapshot.org** — free off-chain voting (no gas fees)
- **Gnosis Safe** — group-controlled treasury (requires multiple members to spend)
- **Raspberry Pi** — could host the guild's website, chat bridge, or documentation

## 4. Investment Track Rules (Critical)

If the group pools money, protect everyone:

- **Minimum contribution** — Example: $25/month (accessible, but meaningful)
- **Multi-signature wallet** — No single person can spend group funds (need 2-of-3 or 3-of-5 members to approve)
- **Snapshot voting** — Every investment requires group vote BEFORE funds move
- **Transparency** — All wallet addresses public; all votes on-chain
- **Exit clause** — Members can withdraw their proportional share with notice
- **Not financial advice** — Every meeting starts with: "This is education, not financial advice. Only invest what you can lose."
- **Legal disclaimer** — Not an investment club in the SEC sense (educational guild that happens to pool funds; consult lawyer if growing large)

## 5. Education Track Curriculum (Draft)

**Week 1:** What is blockchain? (No crypto needed)
**Week 2:** Wallets — Hot vs. Cold, Seed phrases, Tangem cards
**Week 3:** Bitcoin basics — Store of value, Halving cycles
**Week 4:** Ethereum & Smart Contracts — dApps, Gas fees
**Week 5:** DeFi basics — How lending pools work (no investment yet)
**Week 6:** Security — Scams, Phishing, Multi-sig
**Week 7:** DAOs & Governance — How groups make decisions without a CEO
**Week 8:** Building track opens — Start small projects

## 6. Launch Plan

**Phase 1 — Soft Launch (Month 1)**
- Announce in neighborhood/community channels
- First meeting: "What is blockchain and why does it matter to our community?"
- No money collected yet. Education only.

**Phase 2 — Core Members (Month 2)**
- 5–10 committed members
- Set governance rules together
- Test Snapshot voting with a fake proposal

**Phase 3 — Treasury (Month 3)**
- Open Gnosis Safe wallet
- First pooled contribution if the group votes YES
- Start with very small amount ($50–100 total)

**Phase 4 — Projects (Month 4+)**
- Building track starts
- Maybe a neighborhood NFT, a local directory on blockchain, or a community token
