# Commodity Division Expansion Plan

## 5 New Divisions

### 1. Hardware & Tools
- Fasteners, hand tools, power tools, safety equipment
- Target vendors: surplus wholesalers, Harbor Freight bulk, local distributors
- SKU prefix: HW-
- Categories: FASTENERS, HAND_TOOLS, POWER_TOOLS, SAFETY, CONDUIT_FITTINGS

### 2. Solar & Battery
- Solar panels, inverters, battery storage, mounting hardware
- Ties into Think Energy division
- SKU prefix: SB-
- Categories: SOLAR_PANELS, INVERTERS, BATTERIES, MOUNTING, CHARGE_CONTROLLERS

### 3. HVAC Parts
- Ductwork, thermostats, compressors, refrigerant tools, filters
- Ties into electrical contracting (690, 697)
- SKU prefix: HV-
- Categories: DUCTWORK, THERMOSTATS, COMPRESSORS, REFRIGERANT, FILTERS

### 4. Smart Home & IoT
- Smart switches, panels, sensors, controllers, EV chargers
- High margin, growing market
- SKU prefix: SM-
- Categories: SMART_SWITCHES, SMART_PANELS, SENSORS, EV_CHARGERS, CONTROLLERS

### 5. Electrical Supply (formalize existing)
- Wire, breakers, panels, conduit, boxes, connectors
- Already core business, needs vendor diversification beyond TDG
- SKU prefix: EL- (existing TDG/Blacktech items migrate)
- Categories: WIRE, BREAKERS, PANELS, CONDUIT, BOXES, CONNECTORS

## Vendor Pipeline Needed
Each division needs 2-3 vetted wholesale/surplus vendors minimum:
- Hardware: Fastenal, McMaster-Carr surplus, local hardware wholesalers
- Solar/Battery: SunWatts, EcoFlow wholesale, Solergy
- HVAC: SupplyHouse.com, Johnstone Supply, local HVAC wholesalers
- Smart Home: Ring wholesale, Lutron, Leviton commercial
- Electrical: TDG (existing), Grainger surplus, local supply houses
