# Rich Dad — Velocity of Money (Key Numbers)

**Source:** rich_dad_velocity_of_money.pdf (10-page diagram chapter, OCR'd 9/15/26)
**Core idea:** Money loops through 10 steps instead of stopping at a paycheck — each loop makes the same dollar work multiple times.

## The 10-Step Loop
| # | Step | Key number |
|---|------|-----------|
| 1 | Raise capital | $250,000 from investors to fund Rich Dad Co. |
| 2 | Reinvest into business | Reinvested money is **tax-free** (hires, equipment, growth) |
| 3 | Repay investors | ($ × 4) = **400% return in 2 years** = $1M returned |
| 4 | Owners finally pay themselves | Paychecks/bonuses — first taxed money out, ~3 years in |
| 5 | Tithing | **10% of gross** to registered charities — spiritual first, tax deduction second ("god is our partner — if we don't pay our partner, our partner stops working") |
| 6 | Real estate | ($ × 4) = down payment + 4x bank debt; the debt makes up for the tax liability |
| 7 | Government tax deduction | ($ × 50%) = **phantom cash flow** — depreciation shelter |
| 8 | Oil & gas investments | Cash flows into wells |
| 9 | Oil/gas tax deduction | ($ × 80%) deduction → **32% return** in reduced taxes ($1,000 × 80% × 40% bracket = $320) |
| 10 | Gold & silver | NOT an investment (taxed high) — kept as **hedge/insurance** against dollar collapse (Weimar 1923 reference) |

## Worked Tax-Cascade Example (his $1,200 tax bill)
- Start: $1,200 owed
- − $500 real estate depreciation (step 7) → $700
- − $320 oil & gas deduction (step 9) → **$380 final**
- Lesson: "On the B- and I-side of the quadrant, the more I invest, the less I pay in taxes."

## Numbers Worth Remembering
1. **400% / 2 years** — investor return before owners took a dollar
2. **~3 years** — how long Kiyosaki waited before pulling money out (pay yourself last)
3. **10%** — flat tithe on gross, before lifestyle
4. **80% × 40% = 32%** — oil & gas IDR deduction math (2026 note: this is the old pre-OBBBA framing; post-7/1/26 deal terms differ)
5. **$570 of a $1,200 bill** — total tax crushed by deductions (47%)
