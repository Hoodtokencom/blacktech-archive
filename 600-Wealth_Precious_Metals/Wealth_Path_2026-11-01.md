## The correction that drives everything
**$1,500/month = IUL premium. It is paid first, every month, before any other dollar moves.**
That means the emergency fund is NOT built from $1,500/mo anymore. It is built from the small
streams plus the policy's own loan capacity. The whole path is arranged around the premium,
not the reverse.

Why the premium can go first: an IUL is front-loaded. The early years build the death benefit
and the loan capacity, and a lapse in year 1–3 destroys the value of everything already paid
(surrender charges + lost compounding). Paying it late or missing it is the single most
expensive thing on this page.
